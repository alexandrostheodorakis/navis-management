Operational
Precision

Navis Management was established with a clear focus: to provide shipowners with dependable shore-based support and a practical approach to vessel management.

Our team brings together maritime experience, technical knowledge and close attention to detail. We believe effective ship management is built on clear communication, disciplined planning and long-term relationships with owners, crews and trusted partners.

From day-to-day operations to technical matters and regulatory requirements, we work alongside our clients to keep their vessels safe, compliant and commercially focused.

Our Core Services

Comprehensive ship management services tailored to the operational, technical and commercial needs of shipowners and operators worldwide.
